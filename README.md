# The Day's News: Wednesday, 25 November 2026

A code-drawn newspaper front page summing up the world news of
Wednesday, 25 November 2026. The image is `entry.png` (1024 x 1024 PNG);
`draw.py` is the Pillow program that drew it, and `main.ts` copies the
finished image to the output directory. No network is used at run time.

Bounty: https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261125

## The five stories

1. **Asian markets tumble.** Shares fell across Asia on AI doubts and fresh
   US tariff threats: Japan's Nikkei fell as much as 2.9%, South Korea's
   Kospi as much as 5.3%, and SoftBank shed 13.8%.
   Sources: Reuters, "Stocks retreat as AI rebound stumbles, tariff threats
   loom", https://www.reuters.com/world/china/global-markets-global-markets-2026-11-25/
   ; Financial Times (via its Threads post),
   https://www.threads.com/@financialtimes/post/DgJai6njpx_

2. **Japan: services prices at a 36-year high.** The Bank of Japan's
   services producer price index rose 4.2% year on year in October, the
   fastest annual pace since 1990, up from a revised 3.8% in September.
   Sources: Reuters, "Japan's corporate services inflation spikes in
   October",
   https://www.reuters.com/business/japans-corporate-services-inflation-spikes-october-2026-11-25/
   ; Business Today, "Japan's services prices surge at fastest pace since
   1990",
   https://www.businesstoday.com.my/2026/11/25/japans-services-prices-surge-at-fastest-pace-since-1990/

3. **Australia: inflation jumps to 4.9%.** Consumer prices rose 4.9% in the
   year to October, up from 4.3% in September; the Reserve Bank's preferred
   trimmed-mean measure rose to 3.8% from 3.6%.
   Sources: The Wall Street Journal, "Australia's October Inflation Surges",
   https://www.wsj.com/economy/australias-october-inflation-surges-keeping-rba-on-alert-for-further-rate-hikes-7cc40074
   ; HRD / HCAmag, "Inflation jumps to 4.9%",
   https://www.hcamag.com/au/specialisation/change-management/inflation-jumps-to-49-as-wage-growth-falls-behind-what-hr-must-know/598706

4. **Ukraine: strikes hit Odesa and Kyiv.** A Russian strike killed two
   people and injured three, including a child, in the Odesa region, and
   drones hit Kyiv, starting a fire in a multi-storey apartment building
   and injuring two people.
   Sources: Reuters, "Russian strike kills two people in Ukraine's Odesa
   region, governor says",
   https://www.reuters.com/world/russian-strike-kills-two-people-ukraines-odesa-region-governor-says-2026-11-25/
   ; The Independent, Ukraine-Russia war live blog, 25 November 2026,
   https://indy-web-prod.brightsites.co.uk/news/world/europe/ukraine-russia-war-live-nato-estonia-warship-casualties-map-b3090337.html

5. **Philippines: quake shakes Antique.** A magnitude 5.1 earthquake struck
   14 km south-southeast of Valderrama, Antique, at 7:29 a.m. local time,
   at a depth of 10 km; shaking reached Intensity V in Valderrama and
   tremors were felt across Panay, with aftershocks expected.
   Sources: PHIVOLCS-DOST earthquake information,
   https://earthquake.phivolcs.dost.gov.ph/2026_Earthquake_Information/November/2026_1125_020605_B1F.html
   ; Strata News Network, "Magnitude 5.1 Earthquake Strikes Antique",
   https://stratanewsnetwork.com/magnitude-5-1-earthquake-strikes-antique-with-tremors-felt-across-region/

## Research window and coverage notes

Research was conducted on 25 November 2026 between approximately 02:45
and 03:15 UTC (09:45-10:15 WIB), from reports published up to that point.
Only events that happened, or were first reported, on 25 November 2026 in
some time zone are shown:

- The Ukraine strikes were reported late on Tuesday local time and first
  carried by Reuters on 25 November; they are shown as reported that day.
- The Antique earthquake struck at 7:29 a.m. Philippine time on
  25 November (23:29 UTC on 24 November); it is a 25 November story in
  local time.
- Events later in the day on 25 November fall outside this window and are
  not shown: the conclusions of the EU-Western Balkans summit in Brussels,
  the Davis Cup quarter-final between Italy and South Korea, the evening
  Champions League matches, and any US tariff announcement. Nothing about
  them is claimed in the image.
- Near-miss stories checked and left out because they happened or were
  first reported on 24 November: the Takaichi-Akane ICC meeting in Tokyo,
  the BJP-SAD alliance announcement, the Keio ransomware final report,
  Microsoft's "Code Red" memos, and the Thanksgiving turkey pardon.
